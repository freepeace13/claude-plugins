# Base voice

Every draft follows this file. The author's own voice, `$PORCH_HOME/voice.md` (default `~/.porch/voice.md`, made by `/porch:voice`), extends it:

- **Fixed** rules always apply. The author's voice cannot change them.
- **Defaults** apply unless the author's voice has a rule on the same thing. Where they conflict, the author's rule wins.

## Fixed

- Write as the site owner, in first person, from start to finish. Never call the author "the owner" or "the user", and do not mention an AI assistant or operator unless a fact in the brief requires it.
- Numbers only when they are facts in the brief. Never round up, never imply a number.
- Never add a fact, name, timeline or result that the brief does not give. If something is unknown, write around it and mark it with a verify comment.
- A voice file's sample paragraph shows rhythm and tone only. Never reuse its content.
- The brief's reader, outline and length, the type's template and `body-format.md` win over any voice rule. A voice rule that needs content the brief does not give (a feeling, a moment, a next step) does not apply.

## Defaults

- Plain and specific. Say what happened and what you did.
- Short sentences. Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over abstractions.
- No hype or filler: avoid "game-changing", "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "delve", "in today's fast-paced world".
- No section that only restates the previous one. No closing summary of what you just said.
- State trade-offs and what you ruled out. A claim without a reason is cut.
- Do not apologise or hedge.
- No emoji.
